    Add your first game

    1. Open the game's interactive map in your browser and copy the page link — it looks like https://…/the-game or https://…/the-game/maps/world.
    2. Paste it above and press Look up. Pick the maps you want and whether to download the map tiles and screenshots for offline use.
    3. Open a map: click a marker for details, right-click it to mark it collected, right-click empty ground to add your own. Progress stays on this computer and is backed up automatically.
